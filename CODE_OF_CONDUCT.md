# Code of conduct

WallShift welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, keep criticism focused on the work, and allow others room to ask questions and learn.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, and sharing someone else's private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content and limit participation when needed to protect the project community.

Use GitHub's **Report content** controls for abusive behavior. Include enough context for a reviewer to understand what happened and avoid sharing other people's private information.
