# Changelog

## [1.0.0](https://github.com/kidrainzboy/WallShift/releases/tag/v1.0.0) — 2026-11-22

First public release.

- Desktop drag-and-drop widget for static images, timed slideshows, and muted H.264 video wallpaper.
- Per-display or all-display targeting, with six fit modes.
- Double-click to restore the default wallpaper, undo the last change, and capture a still frame from a video.
- Movable and resizable glass widget with a smooth outline and a distinct drop indicator.
- Tray controls, English and Vietnamese localization, and optional launch at sign-in.
- Windows x64 executable and SHA-256 checksums, alongside the source archive.
